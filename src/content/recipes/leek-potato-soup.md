title: "Leek & Potato Soup"
description: "Our go-to after a wet walk."
image: "/recipe-images/leek-potato-soup.svg"
source:
  name: "Hartley family recipe"
addedBy: "Priya"
addedOn: 2026-10-28
category: "Light bites & lunch"
tags: ["Veggie", "Batch cook"]
prepMins: 10
cookMins: 30
serves: 4
ingredients:
  - "3 leeks, sliced"
  - "500 g potatoes, diced"
  - "25 g butter"
  - "1 litre vegetable stock"
  - "100 ml single cream"
steps:
  - "Soften the leeks in the butter for 10 minutes."
  - "Add the potatoes and stock and simmer for 20 minutes."
  - "Blend until smooth, stir in the cream and season."
