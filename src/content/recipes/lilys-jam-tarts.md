yieldText: "Makes 12"

ingredients:
  - "225 g plain flour"
  - "110 g butter"
  - "3 tbsp cold water"
  - "12 tsp strawberry jam"
steps:
  - "Rub the butter into the flour, add the water and squish it into a ball."
  - "Roll it out and cut circles. Press them into a bun tin."
  - "Put a teaspoon of jam in each one (not too much!)."
  - "Bake at 200°C / 180°C fan for 12–15 minutes. Let them cool – the jam is HOT."
