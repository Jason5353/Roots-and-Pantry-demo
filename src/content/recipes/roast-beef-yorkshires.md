ingredients:
  - "1.5 kg beef topside joint"
  - "2 tbsp rapeseed oil"
  - "# For the Yorkshires"
  - "140 g plain flour"
  - "4 eggs"
  - "200 ml milk"
  - "# On the side"
  - "1 kg potatoes"
  - "Salt and pepper, to taste"
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 220°C / 200°C fan. Rub the beef with 1 tbsp oil and season well."
  - "Roast for 20 minutes, then turn down to 180°C / 160°C fan and cook for 15 minutes per 500 g."
  - "Whisk the flour, eggs and milk into a smooth batter and leave it to rest."
  - "Rest the beef under foil. Turn the oven up to 230°C and heat a little oil in a Yorkshire tin until smoking."
  - "Pour in the batter and bake for 20–25 minutes. Do not open the door!"
