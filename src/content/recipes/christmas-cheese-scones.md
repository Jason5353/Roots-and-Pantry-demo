title: "Christmas Cheese Scones"
description: "Made every Christmas Eve for the cheeseboard. Serve warm with chutney."
image: "/recipe-images/christmas-cheese-scones.svg"
source:
  name: "Hartley family recipe"
addedBy: "Priya"
addedOn: 2026-11-10
category: "Baking & bread"
tags: ["Christmas", "Veggie"]
prepMins: 15
cookMins: 15
serves: 8
yieldText: "Makes 8"
ingredients:
  - "225 g self-raising flour"
  - "55 g cold butter, cubed"
  - "100 g mature cheddar cheese, grated"
  - "150 ml milk"
  - "1 tsp mustard powder"
  - "Chutney, to serve"
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 220°C / 200°C fan and line a baking tray."
  - "Rub the butter into the flour and mustard powder until it looks like breadcrumbs."
  - "Stir in most of the cheese, then the milk, to make a soft dough."
  - "Pat out 2 cm thick, cut into rounds and top with the rest of the cheese."
  - "Bake for 12–15 minutes until golden."
