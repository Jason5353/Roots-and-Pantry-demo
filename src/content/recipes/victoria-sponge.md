serves: 8
ingredients:
  - "200 g caster sugar"
  - "200 g butter, softened"
  - "4 eggs"
  - "200 g self-raising flour"
  - "2 tbsp milk"
  - "4 tbsp raspberry jam"
  - "150 ml double cream"
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 190°C / 170°C fan and line two 20 cm sandwich tins."
  - "Beat the sugar and butter until pale, then beat in the eggs one at a time."
  - "Fold in the flour and milk."
  - "Divide between the tins and bake for 20–25 minutes."
  - "Cool, then sandwich with jam and whipped cream."
