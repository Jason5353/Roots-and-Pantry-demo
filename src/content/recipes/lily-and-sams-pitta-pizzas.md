yieldText: "Makes 2"

ingredients:
  - "2 pitta breads"
  - "1 tbsp tomato purée"
  - "50 g grated mozzarella cheese"
  - "½ red pepper, cut into strips"
  - "8 slices pepperoni"
steps:
  - "Ask a grown-up (or Sam) to turn the oven on to 180°C / 160°C fan."
  - "Put the pittas on a baking tray."
  - "Spread the tomato purée all over each pitta, right to the edges, so no bit is bare."
  - "Sprinkle on the cheese. LOTS of cheese."
  - "Make faces with your toppings! Pepperoni for the eyes and a red pepper smile."
  - "Sam puts them in the oven for 8 to 10 minutes, until the cheese goes all bubbly."
  - "Wait a minute because it's REALLY hot, then cut it into triangles."
