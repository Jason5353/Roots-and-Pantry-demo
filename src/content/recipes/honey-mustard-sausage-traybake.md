source:
  name: "Hartley family recipe"
addedBy: "Dave"

category: "Weekend favourites"
tags: ["Quick", "Kids can make"]
prepMins: 10
cookMins: 45
serves: 4
note: "Double the honey if Lily is helping."
ingredients:
  - "8 pork sausages"
  - "2 tbsp runny honey"
  - "1 tbsp wholegrain mustard"
  - "2 red onions, cut into wedges"
  - "600 g baby potatoes, halved"
  - "2 tbsp rapeseed oil"
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 200°C / 180°C fan."
  - "Toss the potatoes and onions in the oil in a big tray and roast for 15 minutes."
  - "Add the sausages and roast for 20 minutes more."
  - "Mix the honey and mustard, brush over everything and roast for 8–10 minutes until sticky."
