tags: ["Kids can make", "Veggie", "Quick"]
prepMins: 10
serves: 2
note: "The best bit is the strawberry on the top!!"
ingredients:
  - "300 g Greek yoghurt"
  - "6 tbsp granola"
  - "100 g strawberries"
  - "50 g raspberries"
  - "50 g blueberries"
  - "2 tsp honey (if you want)"
steps:
  - "Wash all the fruit. Mum cuts the big strawberries in half. Keep the best ones for the top!"
  - "Put the yoghurt, the granola and the fruit in three bowls, with a spoon in each one."
  - "Get a tall glass. Put in a spoon of yoghurt, then a spoon of granola, then some fruit. Do it again and again until it's full to the top."
  - "Put your best strawberry on the very top and sprinkle on a bit more granola. Nana says a tiny drizzle of honey makes it sparkle."
  - "Eat it straight away so the granola stays crunchy!"
